1. Executive Summary

HireGameDev is a specialized hiring platform built exclusively for the game development industry in India. It connects game developers (programmers, artists, designers, QA, audio engineers, producers) with game studios (indie, AA, AAA, outsourcing) through intelligent skill-based matching, verified shipped-title credibility, mandatory salary transparency, and an anti-ghosting enforcement system.

Core Thesis: In game development, what you have shipped matters more than your degree or job title. HireGameDev is the first Indian platform that treats shipped games as the primary credibility signal and builds its entire matching and trust system around it.

Market: India-first. The Indian gaming market is valued at $3.1B (2024), growing at 20% CAGR, with 400+ studios and 50,000+ developers across Pune, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Mumbai, and Chennai.

Technology: ASP.NET Core 8, Razor Pages, PostgreSQL, SignalR — hosted on a Mumbai VPS.

MVP Cost: ~₹620/month infrastructure. 13-week build timeline.

Revenue Target: ₹25L/month by end of Year 1. ₹1.5Cr/month by end of Year 2.

3. Solution Overview

What HireGameDev Is

  • A two-sided marketplace connecting game developers with game studios
  • Intelligent matching based on skills, shipped titles, engine experience, and preferences
  • A credibility platform where shipped games are verified and portfolios are embedded
  • A community hub for game jams, indie teams, and career growth
  • AI-powered onboarding that extracts profiles from resumes, LinkedIn, and portfolios in 2 minutes

What HireGameDev Is NOT

  • Not a generic job board (no IT services, no marketing, no finance roles)
  • Not a recruiter replacement (studios make their own hiring decisions)
  • Not a social network (no feeds, no posts — focused purely on hiring)
  • Not a freelance marketplace in v1 (contract roles yes, escrow-based freelance later)

One-Line Pitch

"India's first hiring platform built specifically for game developers, where your shipped games matter more than your degree."

18. AI-Powered Resume Extraction

Cost: ₹0 for MVP. PdfPig (free, local) extracts text from PDF → Google Gemini (free tier: 1M tokens/day) structures it into profile fields → Form auto-fills → PDF deleted. Total onboarding: 2 minutes.

3 Input Methods:

  1. Upload PDF Resume → PdfPig reads text → Gemini structures → Auto-fill
  2. Paste LinkedIn URL → Fetch public page → Gemini structures → Auto-fill
  3. Paste Resume Text → Gemini structures → Auto-fill

Extracted Fields: Name, Title, Seniority, Years Exp, CTC, City, Skills (engines/languages/tools/genres/platforms), Shipped Titles, Portfolio URLs, Education.

Safety: 2MB max, PDF magic byte check, auto-delete after extraction, no permanent storage.
